Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who uses this app

Under the Headline is used only by its owner to publish videos to accounts the owner controls.

Data we access

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and set their thumbnails on the owner's channel.
  • Instagram: basic profile information and permission to publish Reels to the owner's professional account.
  • TikTok: basic profile information and permission to post videos to the owner's account.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. No data is sold, shared, or sent to any server other than the platforms' official APIs.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's account settings; the owner can delete the local token files at any time. To ask about data, email kamal.bouzekri@gmail.com.
